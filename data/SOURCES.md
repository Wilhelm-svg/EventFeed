# Event sources

All sources checked on 2026-11-22. Facts that were not confirmed are left out of `events.json` and show up in the API's `missingInformation`.

## Southern Right Whale Season
- Primary: https://hermanus-tourism.co.za/things-to-do/whale-watching/ (Hermanus tourism)
- Secondary: https://www.whalehermanus.com/ (local whale-watching operator)
- Confirmed: southern right whales in Walker Bay. Tourism site says June to December. The operator says early June to late November and that the season "closes on 30th November". Stored as June to November, with the disagreement noted in `whatToExpect`.
- Peak months September and October: based on the operator's statement that whales arrive in large numbers in late September and early October. No source gives a numeric reliability, so none is stored.
- Not confirmed: sighting probability on a given day.

## Hermanus Whale Festival
- Source: https://www.whalehermanus.com/ (local operator), corroborated by https://www.capetownetc.com/things-to-do-cape-town/hermanus-accommodation-opens-for-whale-festival-season/
- Confirmed: annual, late September or early October. 2026 edition ran 2 to 4 October 2026. Content (markets, stages, kids' arena, eco-marquees, theatre).
- Not confirmed: 2027 dates (`datesConfirmed: false`), venue, ticketing and price.
- Note: whalefestival.co.za no longer belongs to the festival (it hosts unrelated content), so it was not used.

## Hermanus FynArts Festival 2027
- Source: https://www.hermanusfynarts.co.za/ (official organiser)
- Confirmed: 24 June 2027 to 4 July 2027. Music, art, performance, literature, cuisine. Running since 2013.
- Not confirmed: programme, venues, ticket prices, booking requirements.

## Hermanus Country Market
- Source: https://hermanuscountrymarket.co.za/ (official organiser)
- Confirmed: every Saturday, year round, 09:00 to 14:00. Hermanus Cricket Club, corner of Fairways Avenue and Jose Burman Drive. More than 60 traders. Pet- and family-friendly. Summer Night Markets on Wednesdays 17:00 to 21:00 in December and January.
- Data-quality note: sa-venues.com and foodandhome.co.za list 08:00 to 12:00 and "over 90 traders". Those listings are out of date. The official site was used.
- Not confirmed: entry price (none listed).

## FynArts Old Harbour Sunset Concert
- Source: https://www.hermanusfynarts.co.za/ (official organiser)
- Confirmed: name and date, 6 March 2027.
- Not confirmed: start time, performers, ticket price, booking details. The venue is not stored, even though the name points to the Old Harbour.
