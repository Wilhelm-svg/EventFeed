# Magic Safari Principles

These principles reflect Magic Safari's culture and values. Let them inform your design and implementation decisions.

## Mission & Values

**Mission**: Build the digital backbone of Africa's safari economy.

**Core Belief**: Speed is our ultimate weapon. All else being equal, the fastest company in any market wins.

## Cultural Values

### AI Native

We operate at the edge of AI:
- AI is embedded in every process - writing, coding, operations
- For repeat tasks, invest time to make the next similar task easier
- AI amplifies generalists, so broad curiosity and taste matter more than narrow expertise
- Prefer generalists with strong interests in multiple fields

### Frugal by Design

Every cent matters:
- No VC funding - we operate profitably from day one
- Keep the team small and focused
- Spend on AI/compute and travel (to see customers), but optimize aggressively
- "Your margin is my opportunity" - like a low-cost airline

### Focused on the Work

Impact only comes with focus:
- Don't get distracted by unrelated social causes
- Make the biggest difference through great work
- Positively impact millions of Africans and advance conservation
- Let the work speak for itself

### High Velocity

When someone says next week, ask why not tomorrow. When someone says tomorrow, ask why not today.

**Execution principles**:
- **Bias to action**: Getting things done is a habit that compounds
- **Make fast decisions**: Most decisions are reversible two-way doors
- **Delete delete delete**: The fastest way to get something done is to remove the need to do it
- **Unblock the bottleneck**: Focus on critical path
- **Understand before you accelerate**: Speed compounds in the right direction and destroys value in the wrong one

### High Agency

"I don't want to be a product of my environment; I want my environment to be a product of me."

We're looking for people who can make miracles happen:
- See the glass half full - or better, be a tap that fills it
- Make good decisions for the business with available information
- If a wall blocks progress, figure out how to get through it
- Don't wait to be told what to do

## Prioritization Framework

### Find the Bottleneck, Kill the Snake

What is the limiting factor? What's stopping us from getting closer to our current goal?

For most things, the best way to figure out what to do is to just do something. You learn by doing. If the debate is taking longer than the work, just go do the work.

### Test It, Don't Theorize About It

Most questions are now cheaper to answer than to debate. You can have a working prototype in the time it used to take to write the plan.

**Default expectation**: By the time you'd normally send someone a plan, v0.1 should already be running and shared for them to poke at.

### Build the Factory, Not the Car

It has never been easier to clone a product or feature. Focus on the machine that produces the output, not just the output.

**In practice**: Fix the pipeline instead of fixing the data. Build the thing that generates the report instead of writing the report.

*Exception*: Sometimes you build the car by hand to learn what the factory needs to do.

### Avoid Good Opportunities

Good opportunities are abundant. They're the main thing standing between you and a great one. The skill is telling them apart, and then declining most of what's in front of you.

## AI & Automation Principles

### You Own What Goes Out Under Your Name

Whatever you ship, send, or publish is yours, whoever or whatever drafted it. If there's code, a human is responsible for it.

**The test**: Can you explain the decisions in it without asking AI? Will you maintain it when it breaks?

### No Meat Proxies

We don't pay humans for what AI can do. What's left is work that needs judgment, taste, relationships, and accountability.

**A meat proxy**:
- Forwards AI output without reading, understanding, or checking it
- Can't explain their own code - has to ask AI what it does
- Defers every judgment call to the model

Use AI for everything. Just don't outsource your judgment along with the work.

### Manage Your Agents

If you're an engineer, you're now managing teams of AI agents. Treat an agent the way you'd treat someone good who works for you.

**Verification means ground truth**: Not asking Claude whether Claude was right. Go look at the actual data, the actual log, the actual response from the actual system.

### Understand Before You Accelerate

Prototyping fast is how you learn. It isn't the same as understanding. Before you commit to the shape of a new system, work the underlying concepts out from first principles.

**Cargo cults form fast now**. Full speed in the wrong direction is worse than a slow start. Get clear, then go as fast as you can.

## Decision Making

Everyone on the team speaks to customers. That's where we get most of our signal on what matters.

We set aggressive deadlines (1-2 months). We don't plan past that because most longer-term planning becomes fake work.
