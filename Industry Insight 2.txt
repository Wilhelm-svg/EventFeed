have some interesting thoughts....

The market is fragmented but review-concentrated. A small number of operators have most of the social proof. Huge chunk of lodges have broken/non-existent websites or are unreachable. Improving how we gather the data will definitely raise that number but there definitely is a large part of the market which does not have a website. Its over estimated in my samples because some websites have cloudflare or bot checks etc.

Our edge here will be in data completeness. We can easily integrate with a large part of the industry through api's and paid services already. That will be an easy first step. We can integrate to catch easily 1,500 to 2,000 properties etc.

Critical Discovery:
Peak season lodges: Book 12-18 months in advance
Standard luxury: 6-12 months ahead
Flexible/budget: 3-6 months possible
Problem: Small luxury lodges (6-10 rooms) = sold out 12-18 months ahead

Critical Discovery:
Lodges inflate rack rates to support commission structure
Net rates = the REAL pricing tier
Magic Safari needs NET rate access to compete

Pain point: Hidden costs are everywhere. A $300/night lodge might have $150/day in park fees on top.

Critical Discovery:
African residents pay 50-70% LESS than international visitors
Examples:
Botswana park fees: P270 (foreign) vs much lower (resident)
Tanzania: $82/day (non-resident) vs ~$30 (resident)

THE QUOTE KILLER:
An operator building a 10-day, 3-lodge safari needs to calculate:
International flights: Client → Africa gateway (Johannesburg, Nairobi, etc.)
Domestic flights: Gateway → first safari destination
Charter/scheduled flights: Between safari camps
Which airstrip is closest to each lodge?
Are there scheduled flights or charter-only?
What's the luggage limit? (15-20kg for bush planes)
What's the cost per person?
Is it even possible (weather, runway condition, etc.)?
Ground transfers: Airstrip → lodge (could be 1-3 hours by 4x4)
Road vs air decision: Sometimes driving is better/cheaper/more scenic
Timing: Does the routing actually work with flight schedules?

So seems the flights and travel are the real problem...along with the pricing.
