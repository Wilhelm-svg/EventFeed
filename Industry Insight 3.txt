THE REAL PROBLEM: Multi-Modal Journey Planning with Time Windows
safari routing problem is a specific instance of:

Multi-Modal Transportation Network Design with Time-Dependent Costs and Capacity Constraints

This brings me to the final structure of what I think the plan should look like:

Data Harvesting Engine
Objective: Build the most comprehensive, accurate and up-to-date database of the African safari travel ecosystem.
-connect with third party integrations and API's to capture 20-30% of dataset.
-Employ mass scraping to get the rest
-Going to need have an ever growing "playbook" so that the harvesting engine will find future harvesting easier
-Need to keep data and rates up to date
-So we need to integrate as much as we can then scrape the rest.

Client Requirements & Intake Platform
Objective: Understand exactly what the traveller wants and translate their requirements
-let travel agents fill this in via Magic Safari portal, email or WhatsApp.
-Maybe we should also let the actual travelers fill this in? find their own itineraries? Dual market with capturing travelers and travel agents?

Itinerary Building & Destination Matching Engine
Objective: Identify the best combination of destinations, accommodation, activities and experiences to satisfy the client's requirements.
-understand the client/travelers requirements
-source locations and activities that match client needs
-string them together and connect the dots. Multiple locations, activities etc.

Transport Feasibility & Travel Logistics Engine
Objective: Determine whether each proposed itinerary can actually be travelled, and identify the best way(s) to connect every destination.
-we have the information we need, time to string together the travel
-flights, drives, times, visas, requirements, availability etc.
-make sure we have itineraries and matching travel plans that WORK TOGETHER.
 
Itinerary Evaluation, Ranking & Recommendation Engine
Objective: Evaluate all viable itinerary and transport combinations and present the client with the best three or four options.
-once we have established several itineraries and travel plans, we can rank them, and present the top 3/4/5.
-the client/traveler can then pick what combo of itinerary and travel they want.

Booking & Supplier Orchestration Engine
Objective: Convert a selected itinerary into actual reservations, confirmed services and a complete travel booking.
-once a client/traveler has picked an itinerary and their travel plan, transform into actual reservations and book.

Simple right we just have to do this across multiple borders, across multiple jurisdictions, across multiple traveller profiles and nationalities haha. (joke)

Anyways, I imagine this is probably very similar to the roadmap the Magic Safari team has already been working towards. But I think that's the interesting part: having spent the last few days digging into the industry, I've independently arrived at much the same overall structure and way of thinking about the problem.
